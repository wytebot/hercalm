About HerCalm

HerCalm is a lightweight wellness and self-care toolkit for planning, reflection, relaxation, timers and personal journaling.

Built for customization

This source uses vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript with an optional server-side AI endpoint, making it easy to rebrand, extend and deploy.

Our approach

HerCalm is designed for general wellness and personal organization. It does not present itself as a diagnostic or treatment product.
