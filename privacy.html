HerCalm Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Local Data

The included tracker, mood entries, notes and water counts are stored in this browser's local storage. Core local tools do not require an account or cloud database.

AI Requests

If you use CalmPlan AI, the selected mood, goal, time and optional note are sent to the deployment's server-side AI endpoint. That endpoint forwards the submitted information to the AI provider configured by the deployment owner. Do not enter sensitive information that you do not want processed by that provider.

Third-Party Links

Optional shopping links can open third-party websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices and terms.

Deleting Local Data

Use the app's delete-data control to remove HerCalm's locally stored entries from the current browser. Data already sent to the configured AI provider is not erased by clearing local storage.

Analytics

The included source does not add an analytics service by default. If a deployment owner adds analytics, advertising, authentication or other third-party services, this policy must be updated accordingly.

Contact

Before commercial deployment, replace the placeholder contact with the operator's real privacy contact address.
