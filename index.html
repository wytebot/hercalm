Wellness Guide + FAQs

Gentle Ideas for Your Period Days

  • ✓ Move gently: Try walking or easy movement if it feels comfortable.
  • ✓ Take a pause: use a short timer for quiet rest
  • ✓ Eat normally: Choose balanced foods that fit your usual routine.
  • ✓ Keep it simple: Choose familiar foods and routines you already enjoy.
  • ✓ Hydration: keep a drink nearby and sip according to your normal routine

Keep Your Routine Comfortable

  • • Listen to your preferences: Choose drinks and activities that suit you.
  • ✗ All-or-nothing routines: skip anything that does not feel comfortable
  • • Skip anything uncomfortable: You do not need to force a routine.
  • ✗ Overcomplicating your routine: choose one or two simple activities
